agentic-helpers v2.29.0 is released and carries the removal of its grounding gate and `agentic-capture`, after sdd-helpers v1.0.0 (20261107-104948-s-tac-qzy), as the removal-order decision (20261107-095434-d-tac-26c) required.

The release is tag `v2.29.0` on commit `346b0aa`, published on `fza/agentic-helpers`, and the removal commit `dccf21e` is among its ancestors. Its darwin arm64 archive holds `agentic-helpers`, `agentic-memory`, `agentic-scratch`, `agentic-tools` and `agentic-shell-guard`, and neither removed program. The release also carries the session-start line naming sdd-helpers in graph projects without it, which is part of `dccf21e`.
